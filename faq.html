Services & Solutions

What's the difference between RPA and AI automation?

RPA (Robotic Process Automation) handles rule-based, structured tasks, while AI automation can:

  • Process unstructured data (emails, documents, images)
  • Make intelligent decisions based on context
  • Learn and improve over time
  • Handle complex, variable scenarios
  • Understand natural language

We combine both approaches for maximum effectiveness.
